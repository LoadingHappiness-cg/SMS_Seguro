# SMS Seguro Store Assets

Practical shotlist and asset requirements for the Google Play listing.

## General capture rules

- Use real app UI, not mockups
- Keep screenshots in PT-PT for the Portugal listing
- Avoid showing personal phone numbers, real SMS content, or noisy notification trays
- Use consistent device frame or no frame at all
- Keep status bar clean
- Prefer vertical phone screenshots for the Play listing
- Make sure each screenshot has one clear message only

## Screenshot ordering

### Screenshot #1

- Screen: Protection overview / setup status
- What must be visible:
  - `SMS Seguro`
  - `Proteção contra burlas por SMS`
  - `Proteção ativa` or the main protection card
- Why it matters for Play review:
  - Establishes the app’s core purpose immediately
  - Shows the app is a protective tool, not a generic utility

### Screenshot #2

- Screen: Activation / notification access disclosure
- What must be visible:
  - `Ativar proteção automática`
  - the disclosure bullets explaining what is read and that processing is local
  - `ATIVAR PROTEÇÃO AGORA`
- Why it matters for Play review:
  - Demonstrates transparent disclosure before enabling Notification Access
  - Supports policy review for sensitive notification access

### Screenshot #3

- Screen: Setup status with key checks
- What must be visible:
  - `Acesso às notificações`
  - `Permissão de notificações`
  - `Proteção em execução`
  - `Notificação visível`
- Why it matters for Play review:
  - Shows the app uses visible, user-facing protection
  - Reinforces that monitoring is explicit and status-driven

### Screenshot #4

- Screen: Suspicious SMS alert
- What must be visible:
  - `Alerta de segurança`
  - risk level such as `Risco médio` or `Perigo elevado`
  - a factual warning, never exaggerated
- Why it matters for Play review:
  - Proves the core user benefit
  - Shows the app warns about suspicious content without scareware language

### Screenshot #5

- Screen: Security check / alert details
- What must be visible:
  - `Verificação de segurança`
  - `Motivos da análise`
  - a visible suspicious link or payment warning example
- Why it matters for Play review:
  - Shows explainability, not just alarmist output
  - Helps reviewers understand the app’s analysis model

### Screenshot #6

- Screen: History / alerts list
- What must be visible:
  - `Alertas`
  - at least one realistic previous alert item
- Why it matters for Play review:
  - Shows ongoing usefulness and user reviewability
  - Reinforces that the app keeps understandable local history

### Screenshot #7

- Screen: Rules/update area inside setup
- What must be visible:
  - `Proteção (regras)`
  - `Versão`
  - `Procurar atualizações agora`
- Why it matters for Play review:
  - Shows the app is maintained and its detection logic is updated
  - Supports trust and quality perception

### Screenshot #8

- Screen: Xiaomi / MIUI guidance
- What must be visible:
  - `Telemóvel Xiaomi detectado`
  - battery or autostart guidance
  - `Guia especial para telemóveis Xiaomi`
- Why it matters for Play review:
  - Shows OEM-specific support and practical user help
  - Helps justify persistent monitoring behavior on restrictive devices

## Recommended screenshot flow summary

1. Core value
2. Disclosure and consent
3. Protection status
4. Real warning
5. Detailed explanation
6. History
7. Rules and updates
8. Xiaomi help

## Feature graphic

- Size: `1024 x 500`
- Format: PNG or JPG
- Keep text minimal
- Do not overcrowd with UI
- Focus on brand + outcome

### Suggested minimal copy

- `Proteção simples contra burlas por SMS`

Alternative shorter line:

- `Alerta antes do clique`

### Suggested visual direction

- Brand mark on the left
- Clean phone silhouette or subtle alert motif on the right
- High contrast background
- No tiny UI text

## 512 icon

- Size: `512 x 512`
- Format: PNG, 32-bit
- Transparent background allowed only if the export still reads clearly on light and dark backgrounds
- Export from the final launcher icon source, not a screenshot crop
- Keep safe margins so the symbol stays legible at small sizes

## Asset export checklist

- 8 screenshots exported and named in order
- Feature graphic exported at `1024x500`
- Store icon exported at `512x512`
- No personal data visible
- No debug labels or placeholder text
- Copy matches the actual app behavior in the current release
